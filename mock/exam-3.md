**Covers:** Topics 1 to 11. All four lectures. Questions are shuffled across topics and lean on "which is NOT", "match the pair" and short calculations.

**How to use it:**
- Closed book. No notes, no cheat sheet.
- The timer is 40 minutes. One minute a question.
- Click an answer. It marks itself. Open the notes from the chips under any answer you got wrong.
