**Covers:** Topics 6 to 11. The B group: thiamin, riboflavin, niacin, pantothenic acid, B6, biotin, folate. Lectures 14 and 15.

**How to use it:**
- Closed book. No notes, no cheat sheet.
- The timer is 30 minutes. One minute a question.
- Click an answer. It marks itself. Open the notes from the chips under any answer you got wrong.

**Conversions you may need:** 1 kcal = 0.0042 MJ. 1 mg NE = mg niacin + mg tryptophan ÷ 60. µg DFE = food folate + 1.7 × folic acid.
