# Mock Exam 4 — Major minerals, iron and iodine

**Covers:** Topics 12 to 16. Mineral basics, calcium, phosphorus and magnesium, iron, iodine. Lectures 17 and 18.

**How to use it:**
- Closed book. No notes, no cheat sheet.
- The timer is 30 minutes. One minute a question.
- Click an answer. It marks itself. Open the notes from the chips under any answer you got wrong.

**Conversions you may need:** portions = RDA ÷ mg per portion, rounded up. 1 g salt = 0.4 g sodium.
