# Mock Exam 1 — Fat-soluble vitamins

**Covers:** Topics 1 to 5. Vitamin basics, A, K, D, E. Lectures 12 and 13.

**How to use it:**
- Closed book. No notes, no cheat sheet.
- The timer is 30 minutes. One minute a question.
- Click an answer. It marks itself. Open the notes from the chips under any answer you got wrong.

**Conversions you may need:** 12 µg β-carotene = 1 µg RAE. 1 µg vitamin D = 40 IU. α-TE = α + 0.5 β + 0.1 γ + 0.3 α-tocotrienol.
