# Cheat sheet

Every vitamin on one page.

This is what the MCQs test: name, form, source, job, number, deficiency, toxicity.

Learn the cards. Then check yourself against the tables below.

---

## Fat-soluble vs water-soluble (Topic 1)

| | Water-soluble (B group, C) | Fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) |
|---|---|---|
| Absorption | straight into blood | lymph, then blood (chylomicrons) |
| Transport | travel freely | many need carrier proteins |
| Storage | circulate, little stored | stored in fat tissue and liver |
| Excretion | kidneys remove excess in urine | not easily excreted |
| Toxicity | possible at very high doses | likely at very high doses |
| Requirement | needed more often | needed less often |

**Dietary reference values (EFSA):**
**PRI** = enough for virtually everyone. **AR** = enough for half the people. **AI** = used when an AR cannot be set, the average intake of a healthy population.

## The fat-soluble four

| | Vitamin A | Vitamin D | Vitamin E | Vitamin K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Other names | retinol, retinal, retinoic acid; β-carotene = pro-vitamin | D2 ergocalciferol, D3 cholecalciferol; calcitriol = active | tocopherols and tocotrienols; α-tocopherol most active | K1 phylloquinone, K2 menaquinone, K3 menadiol (synthetic) |
| Active form | retinal (vision), retinoic acid (genes) | 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D (calcitriol) | α-tocopherol | hydroquinone form, recycled by epoxide cycle |
| Best sources | liver, sweet potato, spinach, carrots | oily fish (salmon, mackerel), fortified milk, sunlight | seed oils, almonds, hazelnuts | kale, parsley, broccoli, spinach; gut bacteria |
| Unit or number | RAE. 12 µg β-carotene = 1 µg RAE. RDA men 700, women 600, lactation 950 µg | 1 µg = 40 IU. RDA adults 400 IU (10 µg), adolescents 600 IU (15 µg). Older adults 15–20 µg | α-TE. AI 0.4 mg α-TE per g PUFA | AI 70 µg (EFSA), 1 µg per kg, 65–80 µg/day |
| Function | vision (rhodopsin), gene expression (RAR/RXR), growth, reproduction, immunity | calcium homeostasis: ↑ gut absorption, ↑ kidney reabsorption, mobilise bone. | Status test | liver biopsy, dark adaptation, relative dose response | serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D | | prothrombin time, plasma preprothrombin (little Gla) |

## The B group

| | B1 Thiamin | B2 Riboflavin | B3 Niacin | B5 Pantothenic acid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coenzyme form | TDP = TPP (thiamine diphosphate) | FMN, FAD | NAD, NADP | Coenzyme A (CoA) |
| Job | oxidative decarboxylation (pyruvate → acetyl-CoA, α-KG → succinyl-CoA, BCAA); transketolase; nerve Na transport | electron carrier in redox: ETC, β-oxidation, amino acid oxidation, TCA; glutathione reductase | electron carrier (NAD → NADH) in glycolysis, TCA, β-oxidation, ETC; NADPH for synthesis; DNA repair | carries acyl groups: acetyl-CoA into TCA, malonyl-CoA for fatty acid synthesis, cholesterol |
| Sources | pork, fortified cereal, peas, brown rice, whole grains | milk and dairy, eggs, almonds, meat, fortified cereal | meat, poultry, fish, legumes, nuts, fortified cereal; made from tryptophan | everywhere: meat, eggs, yogurt, whole grains, mushrooms, sweet potato |
| Number | PRI 0.07–0.1 mg per MJ (≈ 0.5 mg/day at 2000 kcal) | PRI 1.3–1.6 mg/MJ (as written on slide) | 1 mg NE = mg niacin + mg tryptophan ÷ 60; PRI 1.3–1.6 mg/MJ | AI 5 mg/day |
| Deficiency | beriberi: dry (nerves, legs, paralysis) and wet (heart failure, oedema); Wernicke-Korsakoff in alcoholics | glossitis, cheilosis, stomatitis; cracked lips, sore throat, skin; lower metabolic rate | pellagra: dermatitis, diarrhoea, dementia, death (4 Ds); maize diets | rare: fatigue, GI upset, burning feet syndrome |

| | B6 Pyridoxine | B7 Biotin | B9 Folate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coenzyme form | PLP (pyridoxal phosphate) | biotin, bound to carboxylases | THF (tetrahydrofolate); 5-methyl-THF in blood |
| Job | transamination and decarboxylation of amino acids (GABA); glycogen phosphorylase; ends steroid hormone action | carboxylases: pyruvate carboxylase (gluconeogenesis), acetyl-CoA carboxylase (fatty acid synthesis) | one-carbon transfers: dTMP for DNA, methionine from homocysteine (with B12), neurotransmitters |
| Sources | fortified cereal, chickpeas, potato, chicken, salmon, banana | liver, eggs, fish, wholegrains; gut bacteria | chickpeas, liver, spinach, asparagus, fortified cereal |
| Number | PRI 1.6–1.7 mg/day; 15 µg per g protein | AI 40 µg/day | PRI 330 µg/day, 600 µg in pregnancy; DFE = food folate + 1.7 × folic acid; 400 µg/day supplement before and during early pregnancy; 4 mg if previous NTD |
| Deficiency | rare; mood changes, skin and mucosa, mild anaemia, low immunity | rare; raw egg white (avidin); dermatitis, hair loss, pins and needles | megaloblastic anaemia; neural tube defects (anencephaly, spina bifida) |

## The six formulas

| Equation | Topic |
|---|---|
| µg RAE = µg retinol + µg β-carotene ÷ 12 + µg other carotenes ÷ 24 | 2 |
| AI vitamin K = 1 µg × body weight in kg | 3 |
| µg vitamin D = IU ÷ 40 | 4 |
| mg α-TE = α-toc + 0.5 β-toc + 0.1 γ-toc + 0.3 α-tocotrienol | 5 |
| MJ = kcal × 0.0042; thiamin PRI = 0.07 to 0.1 mg × MJ | 6 |
| mg NE = mg niacin + mg tryptophan ÷ 60 | 7 |
| µg DFE = µg food folate + 1.7 × µg folic acid | 11 |

## One-line memory hooks

- **A:** retin-**ol** alcohol, retin-**al** aldehyde, retinoic **acid**. 1, 12, 24.
- **D:** skin → liver (25) → kidney (1,25). 40 IU in a microgram.
- **E:** the membrane bodyguard. Gives an electron, vitamin C gives it back.
- **K:** K for **K**oagulation. Gla grabs calcium. Babies get a shot.
- **B1:** **thi**amin, **TDP**, **beriberi**, **booze**.
- **B2:** **ribo**flavin is **yellow**, FAD/FMN, sore **mouth**.
- **B3:** niacin = NAD. No niacin = pellagra = 4 Ds. Tryptophan ÷ 60.
- **B5:** pantos = everywhere. Coenzyme **A**. Burning feet.
- **B6:** PLP moves amino groups. GABA. Glycogen.
- **B7:** biotin = carboxylase. Raw egg white steals it.
- **B9:** folate = one carbon. DNA, red cells, neural tube. 400 µg.
