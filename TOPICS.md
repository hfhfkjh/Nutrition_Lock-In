# HNUT10010 / HNUT10020 Human Nutrition 1 — Topic Tracker

**Scope:** Lectures 12 to 15 (vitamins A, K, D, E, B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9). The exam is **multiple choice**.
**Learning outcomes (slide 3):** describe the **function** and **dietary sources** of vitamins, and know the **deficiencies** linked to them.
**What every MCQ will ask:** for each vitamin, its other name, active or coenzyme form, best food sources, function, deficiency disease, toxicity, and the main number (RDA, PRI or AI).

**Weight** = how many facts the slides spend on it. ●●● = must know · ●● = should know · ● = skim · ✗ = skip
