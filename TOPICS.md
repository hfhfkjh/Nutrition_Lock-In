# HNUT10010 / HNUT10020 Human Nutrition 1 — Topic Tracker

**Scope:** Lectures 12 to 15 (vitamins A, K, D, E, B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9) and lectures 17 to 21 (minerals: Ca, P, Mg, Fe, I, Zn, Se, Na, Cl, K, salt). The exam is **multiple choice**.
**Learning outcomes (slide 3):** describe the **function** and **dietary sources** of vitamins, minerals and trace elements, and know the **deficiencies** linked to them.
**What every MCQ will ask:** for each vitamin, its other name, active or coenzyme form, best food sources, function, deficiency disease, toxicity, and the main number (RDA, PRI or AI). For each mineral (lecture 21, slide 55): its major function, whether a food is a high source, which hormone or transport protein handles it, and how many portions of a food reach the RDA.

**Weight** = how many facts the slides spend on it. ●●● = must know · ●● = should know · ● = skim · ✗ = skip
